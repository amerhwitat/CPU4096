# Crypto Research Links

This repository's wide-register research can provide synthetic cryptographic workloads for Chimera II C8192/R8192 experiments.

## Standards and references

- BIP-32: https://github.com/bitcoin/bips/blob/master/bip-0032.mediawiki
- ERC-55 / EIP-55: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-55
- ERC-1191: https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-1191
- Bitcoin Core secp256k1: https://github.com/bitcoin-core/secp256k1
- bitcoinjs/bip32: https://github.com/bitcoinjs/bip32
- scure-bip32: https://github.com/paulmillr/scure-bip32

## Chimera mapping

Potential synthetic workloads include wide modular arithmetic, SHA-2/SHA-3/Keccak, serialization and secp256k1 accelerator-contract benchmarks. This repository does not add public-address-to-private-key recovery or wallet-targeting functionality.

See the canonical provenance catalogue in https://github.com/amerhwitat/ChimeraIIOS/blob/main/docs/CRYPTO_UPSTREAMS_AND_PROVENANCE.md.
